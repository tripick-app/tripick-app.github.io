# Tripick

Tripick helps turn a big collection of travel photos into a smaller set of moments worth revisiting. On iPhone, it groups photos by capture time and uses location when a photo includes it. On-device photo signals help surface clear images, good expressions, burst representatives, and similar photos.

You choose the photos to organize and how many to keep. Review and adjust Tripick's suggestions, then confirm before it creates or merges a curated album and marks selected photos as favorites in Apple Photos. Tripick does not delete or alter your original photos.

## Privacy

Photo analysis happens on your iPhone. Photos, thumbnails, and analysis results are not uploaded to Tripick servers or third-party cloud AI. Changes to Apple Photos happen only after you review and confirm.

## About this website

This website introduces Tripick and shows its curation flow. The travel scenes are generated examples, not photos from real users; the Tripick interface shown is a product preview.

# Tripick（中文）

Tripick 帮你从大量旅程照片里，整理出一组值得回看的瞬间。它会在 iPhone 上按拍摄时间归拢照片；照片带有地点信息时，也会参考这些线索。设备本地的画面分析会协助发现清晰照片、人物状态较好的画面、连拍代表照和相似照片。

你可以选择要整理的照片和保留数量，检查并调整 Tripick 给出的候选，再确认是否在 Apple Photos 中创建或合并精选相簿、标记收藏。Tripick 不会删除或改动原始照片。

## 隐私

照片分析在 iPhone 本地完成。照片、缩略图和分析结果不会上传到 Tripick 服务器或第三方云端 AI。只有经过你的检查和确认，Tripick 才会在 Apple Photos 中执行更改。
